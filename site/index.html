Whether you are a new or an existing patient, we would like to thank you for choosing our Manchester based practice. At Cleggs Lane Dental, we provide high-quality treatment in a friendly environment. We are a mixed dental practice offering both NHS and private treatments which are tailor made to suit you.

We are also committed to continuing dental education and keeping abreast of the changes in modern dentistry. By engaging positively with our patients we encourage preventive techniques - helping you to understand how to maintain the health of your mouth and keep it free of tooth decay and gum disease.
